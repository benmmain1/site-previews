BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few helpful
answers.

A little detail makes it easier to talk through the work you have in mind.

Ask about your project ↗
Can I discuss several small jobs together?+

Share the full list at the start, including your priorities. Ask which tasks are a fit, whether they can be grouped and how the work will be estimated.

Who supplies the materials?+

Mention anything you have already purchased and ask what else is needed. Confirm who will source the materials and how their cost will be included.

How do I know if a task is a fit?+

Describe the work and the property. Ask which tasks are a fit and whether any part of the project requires a specialist or a separate licensed trade.
