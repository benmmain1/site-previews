Ask about your project ↗
What should I include when asking for a quote?+

Share the approximate home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, cleaning priorities and preferred date. Mention any rooms or tasks you want excluded.

Can I ask about a one-time or recurring visit?+

Describe the type of visit you have in mind. Ask about the available cleaning services, what each includes and the scheduling options.

What should I arrange before a cleaning visit?+

Ask about supplies, access, pets and any preparation needed. Mention delicate surfaces or product preferences and confirm how they will be handled before booking.
